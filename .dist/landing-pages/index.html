Why it matters

Users don't want walls of text.
They want answers they can see.

Getting text from a model is easy. Turning that response into something visual, structured, and trustworthy — that's the hard part.

Text-only AI
  • Long responses users don't read
  • Data buried in paragraphs
  • No structure, no visual hierarchy
LuAI with flow-packs
  • Answers rendered as rich UI widgets
  • Structured data shown visually
  • Each domain gets its own card design
Capabilities

Chat + Widgets, as one.

Flow Packs

Each domain ships as an isolated pack with its own tools, prompts, and widget UI. Add new domains without touching the core.

Build-time Wiring

Packs are registered at build time. No runtime discovery, no hidden magic — explicit, type-safe integrations.
